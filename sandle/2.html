About PainJS Architecture

1. Faith-Based Rendering

Forget standard DOM updates. `document.prayToElement()` sends your requests into a probabilistic queue. If your devotion probability is under 0.5, the DOM politely ignores you.

2. Async Variables & Typos

Why assign variables instantly? In `PainJS`, setters return Promises that take seconds to resolve, and occasionally introduce typos to simulate authentic human mistakes.

3. Gaslighting State Engine

State management that actively lies. State will randomly mutate, deny it ever changed, or require explicit sacrificial calls to `Pain.performRitual()` to stabilize.
