The Timeline of the Seahorse Emoji Myth

While people often look for a seahorse emoji on their digital keyboards, official Unicode records confirm that a standard seahorse emoji has never actually been created or released. Here is how the collective memory and internet lore around it evolved:

  • Pre-2018: Users begin noticing ocean-themed expansions on mobile keyboards, leading many to falsely remember a small, orange or blue seahorse icon among marine life.
  • 2018: A formal proposal to add a seahorse emoji to the Unicode standard is officially submitted but ultimately declined.
  • 2023–2025: The topic gains viral attention across platforms like TikTok and Reddit as a classic example of the Mandela Effect, where large groups share a vivid, identical memory of something that does not exist.
  • Present Day: Language models and AI assistants frequently exhibit confusion over its existence due to statistical assumptions, cementing its status as an internet legend.

Did Luke Skywalker Ever Walk on the Sky?

In literal terms within the Star Wars canon, Luke Skywalker never physically walked on the literal sky or clouds like solid ground. However, the phrase "skywalker" is part of his family surname, rooted in the lore of a fictional universe created by George Lucas.

While characters in sci-fi often navigate floating cities (like Cloud City on Bespin) or pilot spacecraft through the upper atmosphere, walking directly on the sky remains a poetic or metaphorical concept rather than an actual event in the films.
